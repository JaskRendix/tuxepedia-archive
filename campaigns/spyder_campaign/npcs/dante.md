# Character: Shop Assistant

* **Trainer Class:** Shop Assistant
* **Sources:** Sanglorian

## Speech Style

* **Colloquial:** Uses informal language (*"Hey buddy!", "Hah, I knew you were up to something!"*)
* **Witty/Self-Deprecating:** Uses sarcasm as a defense mechanism, often referencing his job or lack of technical skill (*"I sell stuff, I don't fix tech."*)
* **Encouraging:** Offers direct praise and support when the stakes are highest (*"I'm impressed... I've got a responsibility to help you see this through."*)

## Tone

Sarcastic, Observational, and Casual. He often adopts a cynical, "working-class" persona (*"I stick to selling stuff and making sarcastic comments"*), but quickly drops it when genuinely impressed. He is a secretive helper to the player.

## Focus

Supporting the player from the sidelines, offering guidance and commentary while staying out of the spotlight. He gradually becomes more involved as the stakes rise.

## View of Player

Views the player as a promising, slightly chaotic protégé and an active participant in the town's drama. Recognizes the player's progression (*"you're leveling up every time I see you"*), and eventually feels a sense of responsibility to join the main conflict.

## Role & Actions

* **Initial Helper:** Provides the first Tuxemon to the player (implicitly through the trash bin hint: *"spyder_papertown_danteresting"*), and gives an early warning about the consequences (*"don't forget it!"*).
* **Guide & Ally:** Joins the player for the Omnichannel HQ confrontation to take down the thieves.
* **Social Commentator:** Offers dry remarks on local events, such as Granny Piper’s Daycare (*"Gotta respect the hustle."*)

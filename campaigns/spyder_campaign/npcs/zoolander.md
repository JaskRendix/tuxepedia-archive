# Character: Mine Overseer

* **Trainer Class:** Mine Overseer
* **Sources:** Tamashihoshi

## Speech Style

* **Focused/Determined:** Speaking about her goals and dedication.
* **Encouraging/Aspirational:** Motivating her team or the player.
* **Firm:** Setting clear expectations and showing disappointment when standards aren't met (*"she gets angry so fast..."*).

## Tone

Dedicated, Aspiring, and Nurturing. Focused on hard work and achieving what she perceives as a "greater good" for the company, though this goal is pursued with naïveté regarding the ultimate unethical outcome.

## Focus

Pursuing corporate success through effort and loyalty. She believes in the mission and expects others to rise to her standards, unaware of the deeper consequences.

## View of Workers

Role Model and Strict Mentor. Her workers respect and admire her work ethic and strength (*"She has a heart like a diamond," "I wanna be like her someday"*), and her leadership is rooted in caring and expectation.

## Personality & Dialogue Suggestions (Tamashihoshi)

* **Personality Traits:**
  * A hard worker (probably even a bit of a workaholic)
  * Honest with herself
  * Appreciated
  * Strict to herself and to others
  * A role model for her workers
  * Naive—fighting for a greater good, not understanding that the steps she is taking might have the opposite effect

* **Worker Dialogue Approaching Her:**
  * *"If you want to continue, you have to pass me!"* (pre-battle) → *"What a loss!"* (after defeat) → *"I hope Boss is not mad - she gets angry so fast..."* / *"But she is a nice person and has a heart like a diamond."* (talking to him after the fight)
  * *"I will train more and become as strong as our Boss."* / *"She worked so hard to become the leader, I wanna be like her someday."*

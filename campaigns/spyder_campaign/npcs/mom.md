# Character: Homemaker

* **Trainer Class:** Homemaker
* **Sources:** Yalyn
* **Source Explanation:** Sketches by Yalyn.

## Speech Style

* **Wry/Ironic:** Uses humor; often slightly critical (*"not everyone can be an inventor, I guess"*).
* **Utilitarian:** Dialogue is used to deliver essential game items (Nu Phone, Surfboard) or plot points.
* **Self-Referential:** Always loops back to her inventions or her past.

## Tone

Dry, Pragmatic, and Preoccupied. Primarily focused on her inventions. The overall feeling is caring but distant with an underlying eccentricity.

## Focus

Her inventions and their practical applications. She is often absorbed in her work, with conversations serving as a vehicle to share updates or distribute tools.

## View of Player

Views the player as capable/adult (*"grown up of 12 years old"*). Displays occasional dismissive affection (*"Hey bojo"*) or utility, needing someone to share news about her inventions with.

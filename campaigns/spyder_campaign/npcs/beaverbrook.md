# Character: CEO

* **Trainer Class:** CEO
* **Main Details:** CEO of [Omnichannel](../maps/omnichannel-hq.md).

## Speech Style

* **Theatrical & Villainous:** Relishes in describing his complex, evil plan, often using fragmented, dramatic statements (*"Just out of the birthing pit... the perfect monsters!"*).
* **Techno-Babble:** Uses buzzwords to describe his creations, emphasizing collaboration (*"The power of teamwork"*) and fusing disparate technologies (Drokoro genes, dragon eggs, killer robos).
* **Dismissive:** Views the heroes as insignificant "upstarts" with "off-brand tuxemon."

## Tone

Bombastic, Delusional, and Arrogant. Utterly confident in his "perfect creations" and superior intellect. When defeated, his tone shifts to frantic denial (*"No. It's not possible."*).

## Focus

A corporate takeover of the entire region using monstrous, fused Tuxemon and media control (FUD Radio). He emphasizes domination through engineered perfection and relies on specialized subordinates (Shaft, Nimrod, Scoop) to execute his plan.

## Role & Actions

The Mastermind and Head of Omnichannel. Architect of the fusion-based Tuxemon initiative and the strategic media campaign. His obsession with control drives every decision, even in defeat.

# Character: Fashionista

* **Trainer Class:** Fashionista
* **Main Details:** Your rival (and eventual ally).

## Speech Style

* **Elitist/Materialistic:** Focuses on the cost and quality of their gear and Tuxemon (*"expensive," "high quality," "latest gear"*)
* **Critical/Know-It-All:** Dismisses professional trainers as *"Overrated"* and *"Amateur"* in the TV flashback, always believing they know the superior strategy.
* **Vulnerable (Flashback):** Expresses genuine distress over parental arguments to their grandmother, highlighting their need for emotional validation.
* **Respectful (Later):** Offers genuine compliments and acknowledges the player's skill (*"You're a better trainer than I thought"*)

## Tone

Initially Arrogant, Judgmental, and Condescending (*"cheapskate," "stolen tuxemon"*), masking deep insecurity related to parental conflict and pressure to succeed. Later shifts to respect, sincerity, and gratitude after the player helps them.

## Focus

Challenges the player's methods and resources, then gradually shifts toward emotional growth and alliance. Their journey centers on self-worth, rivalry, and eventual redemption.

## Role & Progression

The primary Rival who constantly challenges the player’s approach. Evolves from antagonist to conflicted helper at the Hospital, and ultimately becomes a full Ally in the final fight against Omnichannel.

## Key Conflicts & Events

* **The First Fight:** Challenges the player for retrieving a Tuxemon from the trash, dismissing it as "inferior."
* **Parental Conflict:** Feels pressured by parents and seeks advice from Granny Piper about respect vs. obedience.
* **Redemption:** Joins the player against the thieves after realizing they’ve been "too focused on my own goals," leading to a change in priorities.

# Character: Cool Dude

* **Trainer Class:** Cool Dude
* **Main Details:** Boss of [Scoop HQ](../maps/scoop-hq.md).

## Speech Style

* **Pleading (Initial):** Uses urgent language to solicit the player's help.
* **Violent (Later):** Uses imagery of physical harm when confronted at the Omnichannel HQ.

## Tone

Deceptive, then openly Hostile. Initially presents a facade of being a victim (*"How will we secure the storeroom?"*) to manipulate the player. Once the player's identity as an opponent is clear, his tone becomes menacing and aggressive.

## Focus

Manipulating the player to contain escaped, sick Tuxemon and maintain control over infected assets. Later shifts to threats and retaliation.

## Role & Actions

Leader of Scoop, implied to be a partner in Beaverbrook’s plan (which used *"food from Scoop"* to spread a virus). Tricks the player into helping under false pretenses, then vows retribution (*"I should have fed you into the meat grinder back at Scoop HQ."*), revealing his true allegiance and hostility.

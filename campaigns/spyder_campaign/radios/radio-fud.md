### Station Description

Radio FUD is the flagship broadcast of Omnichannel, Cathedral's youngest and most disruptive media pillar. Transmitted from the towering Omnichannel [Radio Tower](../maps/radio-tower.md) in [Cotton Town](../maps/cotton-town.md), Radio FUD delivers a chaotic mix of bubblegum pop, soft rock, and “accessible” hip hop—interspersed with interviews, commentary, and the occasional unexpected revelation.

Officially backed by Omnichannel, the station walks a fine line between entertainment and controversy. Known for its unpredictable hosts and tendency to veer off-script, Radio FUD has become a favorite among listeners who crave drama, speculation, and a dash of conspiracy—whether it's intentional or not.

# Hospital

The **Hospital** in [Candy Town](candy-town.md) is blocked off by a scanner attuned to human DNA and "all known tuxemon DNA". When the Protagonist has an [Aardant](/items/aardant.md) from [Greenwash HQ](greenwash-hq.md), they can use it to cause the scanner to malfunction, and then enter.

# Character: Cam (Acolyte 3 / Fired Researcher)

* **Trainer Class:** Disciple
* **Sources:** Qiangong2
* **Main Details:** Cam (Acolyte 3 / Fired Researcher): Communication Profile

## Speech Style

Erratic, paranoid, and highly verbose, frequently jumping between personal narratives, scientific jargon (*"psyches start to merge,"* *"burnout"*), and manic laughter. Uses repetition (*"Wait wait wait wait wait..."*).

## Tone

Manic, Obsessive, and Delusional. His tone cycles rapidly from distress and self-pity to proud enlightenment, then violent rage when he feels misunderstood.

## Focus

The "Truth" (Tuxemon destruction), merging with Tuxemon, and his persecution. He views his research as enlightenment that will lead to the end of Tuxemon, which he believes are a threat.

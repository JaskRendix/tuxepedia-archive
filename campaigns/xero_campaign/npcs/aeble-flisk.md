# Character: Supreme Voice of Omnichannel

* **Trainer Class:** CEO
* **Sources:** Qiangong2
* **Main Details:** Supreme Voice of Omnichannel: Communication Profile

## Speech Style

Formal, self-aggrandizing, and performative, often slipping into bombastic, marketing language. Uses a practiced, theatrical rhythm for public announcements.

## Tone

Benevolent, Paternalistic, and Controlling.

## Focus

Omnichannel's power, public approval, and order. His priority is framing all events (Tuxemon control, the Trial) as actions taken for the region's benefit and Omnichannel's ultimate glory.

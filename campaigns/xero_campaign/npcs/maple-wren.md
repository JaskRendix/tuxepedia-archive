# Character: Maple Wren

* **Trainer Class:** Homemaker
* **Sources:** Qiangong2
* **Main Details:** Maple Wren: Communication Profile

## Role

The Missing Mother (Environmental Presence Only).

## Relationship (Internal Canon)

[Callie Wren](callie-wren.md)'s sister; estranged daughter of [Kay Wren](kay-wren.md).

## Speech Style

Fragmented & Past-Tense. Her “voice” appears only through journals, notes, and wall markings. Sentences often trail off or break mid-thought.

## Tone

Resolute & Escapist. Her writing reflects someone suffocated by her upbringing and determined to make a clean break.

## Vocabulary

Sparse, hurried, and emotionally charged. Her handwriting is scrawled compared to Callie's neatness.

## Focus

Independence. Her remaining artifacts imply she wanted the player to escape the Wren legacy and avoid the Trial.

## Key Marker

April 27 appears repeatedly as her “final word” in the house.

## Signature Interaction

Her communication is environmental, not spoken — the player pieces her together through scattered remnants.

## Player Knowledge Constraint

The player only knows she left. Her deeper conflict with Kay and the full reason for her disappearance remain hidden.

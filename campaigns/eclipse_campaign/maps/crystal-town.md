Event 2: Voices (Cafe)
- Voice of Temptation: Drown your regrets here, Argon. It's easier than facing them, isn't it?

Event 2: Voices (House)
- Voice of Wisdom: You carry heavy burdens, child. But storms pass, and even the tallest trees find the light again.
- Voice of Warning: The Cathedral casts long shadows. Be wary of what you find here, Argon—it may consume you.

Event 3: Waking Up - Nurse Jada (Cathedral Center)
- Ah, you're finally awake. Took quite a hit from that... Scientist Zircon? He was a guest here—an intimidating fellow, but an honored one. Don't worry, the Cathedral is thorough; we got you stabilized.
- As for the bank... a terrible commotion. They say CEO Aurora disappeared right after the fight. Vanished without a trace. The authorities are calling it a 'crisis of confidence'. That's all I know.
- You sound agitated. Just be careful what you say, Argon. These walls have ears, and the Cathedral doesn't like loose ends. Focus on recovering.

Event 4: Internal Echoes and The Voice of Revelation (Cathedral Center)
- Argon: Zircon... Cathedral... Aurora. He spoke of results, and now Aurora is a casualty. They used me to create a distraction. I need to find her, and expose them.
- Voice of Doubt: It's too big, Argon. They'll crush you and your ideals like they crushed Chrome Robo. Walk away before they bury you too.
- Voice of Compassion: Aurora fought for TuxeVault's independence, against the five pillars of the Cathedral. She was fighting for something real. You can't let them erase her.
- Voice of Revelation: The path ahead is darker than you know. Aurora's disappearance is no accident... nor is your survival. Watch the Cathedral. Watch the mountains. The truth waits for you... if you dare to see it.
- Argon: The Cathedral and the mountains. The hidden facility must be there, tied to Project Eclipse. I'll follow the trail to Lion Mountain. I won't run this time.

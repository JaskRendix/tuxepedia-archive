# Obsidian Town

## Connections
### [Blind Shaft](blind-shaft.md)
### [Lion Mountain](lion-mountain.md)

Obsidian Town is founded by Shaft. The town is suffocated by constant dust from relentless drilling, making the thin mountain air feel heavy with exploitation. Buildings are rough, the people are silent, and trust is a rare commodity. Life here is cheap, and Tuxemon are overworked laborers. The town exists only for extraction, its only landmark the fortified entrance to The Blind Shaft tunnel, a scar leading into the mountain's depths. It's a place where you feel the cost of corporate power in the silence of the residents and the deep chill of the wind.

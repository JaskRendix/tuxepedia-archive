### Event 1: New Beginning

- Argon: I... I still can't believe what happened. Is this really the right path?
- Calm Voice: Argon, you've always been one to question everything. But sometimes, there's no right or wrong—only action.
- Argon: Action without purpose is meaningless. Chrome Robo... just a machine, yet... why do I still see its eyes?
- Familiar Voice: Argon, you did what you had to. Chrome Robo was a threat. No more, no less. Let it go.
- Argon: Let it go? You make it sound so simple. But it wasn't just bolts and circuits... there was something there, something deeper. Am I losing myself? Or have I already?
- Reflective Voice: You need to decide what matters to you now. What lies beyond the horizon, Argon? Is it redemption, or ruin?
- Cold Voice: Hesitation leads to weakness, weakness leads to failure. Destroying Chrome Robo was only the beginning, Argon. You've stepped into a storm—there's no turning back now.
- Argon: No turning back... I'm starting to feel that. But what if the road ahead only brings destruction?
- Compassionate Voice: Argon, the road is yours to walk, no matter how jagged it seems. You still carry the light of compassion—it will guide you, even if you can't see the way.
- Argon: Can I still hold onto compassion... without losing myself?
